Privacy Policy

Last updated:

Manager Lite ("we", "us", "the app") is a football club management app. This policy explains what information we collect, why, and how you can control it.

1. Information we collect

When you create an account, we collect:

  • Email address — for account creation and sign-in.
  • Name — for your profile and squad rosters.
  • Phone number (optional) — for contact within your club.
  • Profile photo — if you choose to upload one.

When you use the app, you and your club members also create club data: squads, formations, matches, training sessions, attendance, and tournament records.

If you sign in with Google or Apple, we receive only the identity token needed to authenticate you — not your Google or Apple password, and not access to your broader account.

2. How we use your information

Solely to provide the app's features to you and your club: managing your squad, scheduling matches and training, tracking stats and attendance, and letting your club's admins and coaches organize the team. We do not sell your data, and we do not share it with advertisers.

3. Where your data is stored

Manager Lite is built on Supabase, which provides our database, authentication, and file storage (profile photos). All data in transit is encrypted (HTTPS/TLS). Supabase acts as our infrastructure processor — it does not use your data for its own purposes.

4. Third-party services

  • Supabase — database, authentication, file storage.
  • Google Sign-In — only if you choose to sign in with Google.
  • Apple Sign-In — only if you choose to sign in with Apple.

5. Data retention & deletion

We retain your account and club data for as long as your account is active. To request deletion of your account and associated data, contact us at managerliteapp@gmail.com. (An in-app "Delete account" option is planned; until it ships, email is the way to request deletion.)

6. Children's privacy

Manager Lite is intended for club admins, coaches, and players managing their own accounts, and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without parental consent.

7. Your rights

You can access, correct, or request deletion of your personal data at any time by contacting us. If you're in a region with additional data-protection rights (e.g. GDPR, CCPA), those rights apply and we will honor requests made under them.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app evolves. Material changes will be reflected here with an updated date at the top of this page.

9. Contact us

Questions about this policy or your data: managerliteapp@gmail.com
